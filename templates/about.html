From Impact Project to School Legend

It all started as an Impact Project at Albany Senior High School — one of those wild ideas that began with a simple question: “What if we could make lunch more awesome?”

A group of students noticed that everyone loved pizza days, but they wanted something that felt theirs — made by students, for students. So they pitched ASHS Pizza, a project that would combine business, design, and culinary creativity.

They began by experimenting in the school kitchen, testing dough recipes between classes and designing logos in the art room. The tech students built a website, the business students handled pricing and marketing, and the food tech crew perfected the toppings.

Soon, the project grew into a full-blown student enterprise. The slogan “Slice of School Spirit!” captured what it was all about — teamwork, creativity, and a dash of fun.

Today, ASHS Pizza isn't just about food; it's a symbol of what happens when students take an idea, bake it with passion, and serve it to the whole community
